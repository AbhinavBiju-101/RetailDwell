How to read this

A stop is staying within 1 m for at least 5 seconds. Stopping is the headline because a display that moves onto a main path is walked past more, which would flatter a count of people who merely came near it.

Comparing shares of shoppers removes the effect of a busier day. Loiterers and people waiting are left out.

This shows association, not proof. The cleanest test compares days of the same type with the same promotions running.
