What if a zone moved?

Pick a shelf zone and see how many shoppers would be likely to stop in it at each open spot on the floor. This is an estimate from the tracks already recorded, not a forecast. It assumes people stop in the zone at the same rate they do today once they walk past it.

How this is worked out, and where it can mislead

  • A shopper "passes" a zone if they spend {{ presence_s }} seconds or more within {{ catch_m }} m of its edge. Stopping in the zone counts.
  • Capture rate is the share of those who pass that go on to stop. The estimate keeps it and re-counts who would pass the new spot, using the same recorded visits.
  • It does not know that a moved zone changes where people walk, that a new neighbour may draw or hide it, or that the space it leaves behind will be used differently. Treat it as a way to rank ideas before trying one, then confirm with a before and after recording.
  • The error figure comes from simulated shops where the rules were known, so it is a best case. Loiterers and staff are left out, and a short recording makes any estimate shakier.
